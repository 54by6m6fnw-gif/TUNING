How to use this with the Terminator X

This app never talks to the ECU. Holley EFI software stays in charge of reading and writing the tune. You move data in and out with CSV exports and copy and paste.

1. Get a good datalog

  • Warm the engine fully. Cold data is filtered out by coolant temp.
  • Drive steady at as many RPM and load points as you can. Hold each for a few seconds.
  • Log measured AFR, target AFR, RPM, MAP, TPS, coolant temp, closed loop comp and learn.
  • In Holley EFI software, open the datalog and export it to CSV. Load that file here.

2. Correct the VE table

  • Copy your current VE table from Holley EFI software and paste it in the VE correction tab.
  • Check the RPM and MAP axes match your table.
  • Press Correct VE table. Cells with enough data change. Grey cells did not have enough data and keep their old value.
  • Press Copy table for Holley, select the whole VE table in Holley EFI software and paste.
  • Repeat: log again, correct again. Each pass gets closer. Use the "Apply this much of the fix" setting to take smaller steps.
  • If you correct with learn included, clear the learn table in Holley after you paste the new VE.

3. Knock

Paste your timing table in Knock and timing. The app pulls the worst retard seen in each cell plus a margin. Always confirm real knock (not noise) before trusting it.

Safety

These are suggestions. Watch AFR and knock on every change, keep WOT pulls short until the tune is proven, and keep a saved copy of every tune you load.
